Training rules

• Bench, squat and deadlift: rest 2–3 minutes.

• Other exercises: rest 60–90 seconds.

• Choose accessory weights that leave 2–3 good reps in reserve.

• Increase weights gradually only when your form stays solid.

• Cardio goal: up to 200–300 calories, but machine estimates vary. Don't force the number.
